The Practice

I always seem to land somewhere in the middle.

I can help you think an idea through, or I can take it off your plate. I can teach you how to do something, work alongside you, or disappear into the details and come back when it’s finished. Some people want to know exactly how the sausage is made. Some would strongly prefer not to. Both are fine with me.
